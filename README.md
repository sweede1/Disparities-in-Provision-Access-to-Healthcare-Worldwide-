##	Which country has the highest prevalence of wasting in children under 5 years of age
<img src="https://github.com/sweede1/Disparities-in-Provision-Access-to-Healthcare-Worldwide-/blob/main/Highest%20Prevalence%20of%20Child%20Wasting.png" alt="Logo" width="800">
India has the highest incidence of wasting in children under the age of 5 years.
According to the data found in a UNICEF study, the patterns for incidence of wasting and severe wasting were similar to prevalence. They linked the issue with lower maternal education, poor nutritional status of the mother, Caesarean section, low birthweight, and male children were associated with higher risk of wasting. 
Wasting during the first six months of life was found to be an independent risk factor for wasting at 12 and 24 months of age.

## Indicators
1. Healthy life expectancy at birth (years)
2. Population with household expenditures on health > 10% of total household expenditure or income (%)
3. Population with household expenditures on health > 25% of total household expenditure or income (%)
4. Life expectancy at birth (years)
5. Density of medical doctors (per 10 000 population)
6. Total net official development assistance to medical research and basic health sectors per capita (US$), by recipient country
7. Prevalence of wasting in children under 5 (%)
8. Proportion of population using safely-managed drinking-water services (%)

It was expected that the different indicators, together  could be used to draw a conclusion and who some countries are facing the health issues they are facing, and we would be able to recommend a remedy on how they can fix the issue base on data to improve standard of living in the respective country(s.)
                       
## Analysis
All of the countries captured in the data are at different stages of development, have their own internal problems and their respective goals as a country. 
There is no single factor that the WHO have collected that can be pinpointed and say for sure this is the reason who this country has this particular problem they are facing.
1. While a lot of countries have their own issues, they all seem to be doing worse in their own indicator of standard of living.
2. The Central African Republic has the lowest access to safe drinking water.
3. India has the highest prevalence of child wasting.
4. Lesotho has the lowest life expectancy after birth with an average of  51.6 years.
